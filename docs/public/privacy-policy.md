This policy explains how Nuvriqo Portal+ for Jira Service Management ("Portal+") handles data. Portal+ is provided by [Nuvriqo legal entity name], [registered address] ("Nuvriqo", "we").

## Summary

- Portal+ runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, Atlassian's hosted app platform.
- We do not operate servers for Portal+, and Portal+ sends no data outside Atlassian.
- Portal+ stores only its own configuration and uploaded logos. It does not store request content or personal data.
- Nuvriqo staff cannot see your Jira data through Portal+.

## Data Portal+ accesses

To show customers their requests and to let administrators configure the app, Portal+ reads the following from your Jira Service Management site, through Atlassian's APIs:

- requests the signed-in customer reported or that are shared with their organisations, and the fields, SLAs and status history configured to be shown;
- project, service desk, request type, status, field and organisation information used in the Portal+ settings page;
- which JSM organisations the signed-in customer belongs to.

This data is processed within Atlassian's platform to display the page and is not retained by Portal+.

## Data Portal+ changes

Only when an administrator enables it, Portal+ can update fields a customer is permitted to edit, move a request to an administrator-chosen status (Close or Escalate), and add an internal comment recording which customer made the change. These changes are stored in your Jira site like any other change.

## Data Portal+ stores

Portal+ stores, in Atlassian's Forge storage for your installation:

- configuration entered by your administrators: experience names, headings and wording, colours, organisation and status selections, request types, help center URL endings, field selections and values, announcements, links, and guide titles and links;
- logos uploaded by your administrators;
- small technical caches (for example, which project a portal belongs to).

Portal+ does not store request descriptions, comments, attachments, customer names, email addresses, account IDs, passwords or API tokens.

## Data sharing and transfers

Portal+ does not send data to Nuvriqo or to any third party. Hosting, storage and data location are provided by Atlassian under your agreement with Atlassian; see Atlassian's privacy policy and data residency documentation.

## Retention and deletion

Portal+ configuration and logos remain in Forge storage while the app is installed. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian's Forge platform handles removal of app storage according to its published retention behaviour. Administrators can delete configuration at any time by removing experiences and logos in the Portal+ settings page.

## Support requests

If you contact Nuvriqo support, we receive the information you choose to send (for example your name, email, site URL and screenshots). We use it only to handle your request and keep it for [retention period]. Please do not send passwords, API tokens or confidential request content.

## Security

Customers can see only requests they reported or that are shared with their organisations; Portal+ enforces this on every request. Access to settings is limited to Jira project administrators. Please report suspected vulnerabilities to [security contact email].

## Your rights

Because Portal+ does not store personal data, requests about personal data held in your Jira site should be made to your Jira site administrator or to Atlassian. For questions about this policy, contact [privacy contact email].
