# Nuvriqo Portal+ support

We help with installing, configuring and using Portal+ for Jira Service Management Cloud.

## How to get help

Email **[support@nuvriqo.com]** or use [support portal URL].

Please include:

- your Atlassian site address (for example `yourcompany.atlassian.net`);
- the service project, and the help center if relevant;
- who is affected: administrators, agents or customers;
- what you expected and what happened, with screenshots or the exact error message;
- steps to reproduce it, and roughly when it happened.

Never send passwords, API tokens or confidential request content.

Many questions are answered in the [documentation](documentation.md), including the troubleshooting table.

## What we support

- Installation, configuration and licensing of Portal+
- Experiences, branding, help centers, services, documents and self-service settings
- Customer-facing behaviour of the Portal+ dashboard, requests, reports and exports
- Defects in the current Marketplace version of Portal+

Problems caused by Jira Service Management configuration or Atlassian platform issues may need your Jira administrator or Atlassian support; we will tell you if so.

## Supported versions

We support the current Marketplace version. Forge apps update automatically for minor versions; major versions may need your administrator to approve an update in Jira.

## Security issues

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to **[security contact email]**. Please don't disclose them publicly until we have fixed them. We follow the Atlassian Marketplace security bug fix policy.

## Feature requests

We welcome ideas. Send them to support with a short description of the problem you want solved.
