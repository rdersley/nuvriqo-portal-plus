# Nuvriqo Portal+ for Jira Service Management

A branded self-service workspace for every customer, on one Jira Service Management project.

Portal+ adds a customer workspace to the JSM portal. Customers see what needs their attention, find and export their requests, check SLAs and progress, update permitted details and read guides written for them. Your team keeps one JSM project, one workflow and one set of permissions.

## What Portal+ does

- **A portal per customer:** experiences with their own brand name, logo, colours, wording, services and guides, mapped to JSM organisations and help centers.
- **Requests customers understand:** dashboard counters, an Action Centre, My Requests with search, filters, saved views, up to 8 columns and live SLA status.
- **Self-service with control:** request detail with SLA and progress timeline, fields customers may update, and Close or Escalate when you allow it.
- **Reports and exports:** created vs resolved, by type and status, SLA performance and resolution time by period; CSV and Excel export.
- **Guides library:** customer-specific guides linked to your knowledge base.
- **Nuvriqo suite:** Approvals and My Assets appear when Smart Approval Manager or Asset Manager is installed.

## Security and data

Portal+ runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, with no Nuvriqo servers and no data sent outside Atlassian. Customers see only requests they reported or that are shared with their organisations; Portal+ checks this on every request. Portal+ stores only its configuration and uploaded logos, not request content or personal data.

## Platform

Jira Service Management Cloud. Current version: 1.0.0.
