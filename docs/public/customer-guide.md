# Portal+ — Customer Guide

Portal+ appears at the top of your service provider's Jira Service Management portal. Sign in to the portal as usual; there is no separate login. What you see depends on how your provider has set Portal+ up, so some sections below may not appear.

## Home

- **Counters:** how many of your requests are open, waiting for you, and waiting for the support team. Click a counter to see those requests.
- **Action Centre:** requests that need your reply, oldest first.
- **Services:** tiles that take you straight to the right request form.
- **Announcements:** notices from your provider, such as planned maintenance.
- **Guides & documents:** guides your provider has published for you, with search.

## My Requests

- See requests you raised and requests shared with your organisation.
- Search by keyword, and filter by status, request type and date.
- Save a view to come back to the same filters later.
- Export the list to CSV, or to Excel where your provider offers it (up to 1,000 requests).
- Where your provider shows it, an SLA badge shows whether a request's target is running, paused, met or breached.

## Request detail

Click a request to open its details:

- the fields your provider has chosen to show, and the request's SLA status;
- a timeline of status changes;
- **Edit:** update fields your provider allows you to change, then save;
- **Close** or **Escalate:** where your provider offers them, close a request you no longer need, or escalate one that needs more attention.

Changes you make are recorded on the request for your provider's team.

## Reports

See your request activity for a chosen period: requests created and resolved, requests by type and status, SLA performance and average resolution time.

## Approvals and assets

If your provider uses Nuvriqo Smart Approval Manager or Asset Manager, you may also see **Approvals** and **My Assets** sections.

## Privacy

You only ever see requests you raised or that are shared with your organisation. For questions about a request, or about what you can see, contact your service provider.
